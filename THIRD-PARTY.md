## Artwork

`assets/rivage-waterlilies.jpg` is a reproduction of a water-lily painting in the
public domain (the artist died in 1926). It is used as the plug-in's background
and is embedded into the binary as binary data. The file is included for
decorative purposes only; no rights in the original artwork are claimed.
